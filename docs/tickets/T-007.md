- [ ] (ADR-0002, supersedes path/shape wording above) routes live under `/v1`: `POST /v1/documents` (multipart `files`, up to 10 files, 10 MB each, 50 MB per request; always 200 with per-file `UploadResult{ok, doc_id, status, deduplicated, error{code, detail}}`; ingestion runs synchronously in the request), `GET /v1/documents` (paginated `limit`/`offset`, returns `total`), `GET /v1/documents/{doc_id}`, `DELETE /v1/documents/{doc_id}` (204; 404 for other owners, never 403; 409 `document_busy` while ingesting), `POST /v1/summarize` (`doc_id` or `doc_ids`, exactly one; returns `{summary, doc_ids}`), `POST /v1/query` (`{question, doc_ids?, top_k?, session_id?, stream?}` -> `{answer, citations[], abstained, session_id?}`; sessions and streaming bodies are accepted but handled in T-020/T-019).
