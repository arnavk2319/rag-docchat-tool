Scope is explicit

Tick one or more documents on the left. The chat header, the placeholder and every answer's citations follow the ticks. Changing the ticks mid-conversation leaves a note in the thread, so earlier answers stay attributable.

One conversation per question set

Each conversation remembers its own document selection. Switching conversations restores it. Documents still indexing cannot be ticked yet.

Evidence beside the answer

Citation chips open the exact passage in the right pane with the cited characters highlighted. On a phone the three panes become tabs.

Palette

Teal carries every action and your own messages. Blue is reserved for citations and links. Amber marks highlighted evidence. Greys do the rest.
